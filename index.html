What this reveals

The model does not choose the next word directly. It assigns a score, called a logit, to many possible next tokens. The app converts the strongest logits into probabilities so you can compare what the model thinks is likely.

After you inspect a prompt, this section will summarize the strongest prediction and explain how changing the prompt changes the distribution.
